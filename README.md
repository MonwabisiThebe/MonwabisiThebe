## About Me

My interests are centred on **Data Science, Machine Learning, and Artificial Intelligence**, particularly how data can be used to investigate complex questions, uncover meaningful patterns, and support informed decisions.

I work with data through exploratory analysis, statistical methods, predictive modelling, and visualisation. I enjoy understanding the reasoning behind models, evaluating their performance, and examining the assumptions and limitations that influence their results.

I'm interested in applying these approaches across different domains, with an emphasis on analytical rigour, interpretability, and practical relevance.

## Selected Projects

### DIRISA 2026 — Electoral Data Analysis & Predictive Modelling

Contributed to a University of Fort Hare team project investigating electoral participation, demographic patterns, and political competition across Eastern Cape municipalities.

The project combines historical election data, demographic indicators, exploratory analysis, machine learning, and scenario modelling to investigate electoral trends and potential outcomes.

**Recognition:** Top 9 qualification · 4th place · 83% assessment score

**Technologies:** Python · Pandas · Scikit-learn · Streamlit
